Architecture

Modern Architecture

Modern architecture emphasizes simplicity, functionality, and clean lines. Architects use materials such as glass, steel, and concrete to create elegant structures that are both aesthetically pleasing and practical. Sustainable design and energy efficiency have become key principles of modern architecture.

Historical Architecture

Historical architecture reflects the culture, traditions, and technological achievements of different civilizations. From ancient temples and castles to Gothic cathedrals and classical monuments, these structures continue to inspire architects around the world while preserving the heritage of humanity.
